# Platform balance update

Platforms use 75% of their previous dimensions. Collectible size is unchanged.
Moving branches share a 0.55-unit travel amplitude; their size-based speed factor grows continuously from 0.62 to 1.48, multiplied by 1.25. This orders peak movement speed by width; each branch still slows naturally at its turning points.
Generation reserves full movement envelopes with a 0.5-unit horizontal margin. Optional branches are vertically staggered 1.1–1.45 units below the route branch. Insertion rejects overlapping envelopes at similar heights.
At pace 2.5 and above every stationary platform becomes fragile, including mushroom platforms (which still launch the player before breaking). Existing platforms update both behavior and cracked appearance.
Jetpacks spawn every 30 seconds of active play in randomly sampled open-air positions, constrained by normal-jump reach from nearby platforms. A fallback ensures the interval is not silently skipped. Flight remains five seconds.

No tests or build executed, per user request. Existing CI remains enabled.
